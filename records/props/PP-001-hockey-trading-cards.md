Hockey trading cards, proposed again by the designer on 2026-10-20 as a core
prop alongside PP-002 in the compact format (PR-005).

Cards could carry player identities, team details, stats and connections that
help players piece together clues from the photos. Matching, ordering and
comparing information are possible mechanics, not selected puzzles. Card count,
dimensions, subjects and release order remain open.

Earlier idea: one card per team, with stats on the back hiding lock digits.
This belongs to the previous lock format; no answer or extraction method is
chosen for the envelope version.

Space Exploration also has trading cards as an idea, so the card mechanic should differ.
