Direction proposed by the designer on 2026-10-20: try a smaller box with flat,
roughly postcard-sized props. The recurring story pieces are photographs with
handwritten messages on the back (PP-002). Trading cards (PP-001) are the other
proposed core prop.

Keep the marked-envelope structure. The photos carry the friend's hockey road
trip story and clues; the cards could help players connect people, teams and
information. Specific puzzle mechanics remain open.

Exact box dimensions, photo and card sizes, quantities, and envelope packing are
not chosen. Fit and readability need a physical prototype. Review the older
bulky prop ideas against this direction before including them.
